REQUIREMENTS

To ensure the highest quality results, please follow these guidelines before submitting your track.

Audio Format
  • WAV or AIFF only
  • 24-bit preferred (16-bit minimum)
  • Sample rate: 44.1 kHz or higher
Headroom
  • Leave -6 dB headroom on your master
  • No clipping
No Limiting
  • Remove any limiter from the master channel
  • Avoid heavy compression on the master
Export Quality
  • Export your final mixdown
  • No MP3 or compressed formats
Mix Balance
  • Proper balance between elements
  • No distorted or over-saturated signals
  • Clean low-end (kick / bass separation)
Version
  • Submit the final version only
  • No drafts or unfinished ideas
Optional (Recommended)
  • Reference track (if you have one)
  • Notes or artistic direction
